About the Studio

Built by a maker,
for makers.

Creative Studio is the web development arm of The Knead & Bloom Co. — a family of ventures rooted in craft, community, and creativity, based in San Diego, CA. Every site the studio builds comes from first-hand experience running a small business on the web.

No bloated platforms. No mystery invoices. Just clean, professional websites built on a stack you'll actually understand — designed to grow with you.
